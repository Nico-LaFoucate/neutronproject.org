Neutron runs Adobe Photoshop on Linux: Photoshop 2026 and 2025, installed straight from Adobe and signed in with your own Adobe account. It's in beta, stable for day-to-day work, and it has been used for paid client work.

## What works

- **The full workspace**, with the canvas drawn on the GPU.
- **Pen tablets**: pressure, tilt, the eraser and the pen's side buttons.
- **Plugins**: filters such as G'MIC-8bf appear under **Filter**, and UXP plugins (`.ccx`) under **Plugins**. Install them from Collider's Plugins page: see [Installing plugins](/wiki/start/basics/plugins/).
- **Copy and paste images** between Photoshop and Linux apps, in both directions, and **drag and drop** files in from your file manager.
- **Edit in Photoshop** from Lightroom Classic.
- **GPUs**: NVIDIA is the tested path. On AMD, Photoshop 2026 uses the GPU. A user reports Photoshop working on Intel Iris Xe.

## Get it running

[Install Collider](/wiki/start/basics/install-collider/), install Photoshop from its Mud Hut tab, then see [Running your first app](/wiki/start/basics/first-app/). Current problems across the apps are listed under [Known issues](/wiki/start/help/known-issues/).

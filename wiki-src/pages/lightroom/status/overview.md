Lightroom Classic is in good shape on Neutron. Confirmed working:

- **Boots and imports at scale** — clean startup, 1000+ RAW files imported at once, SD-card hotplug auto-detected. GPU auto-detected and enabled.
- **Develop performance** — smooth zoom and slider response (the earlier stepping is fixed).
- **AI features run natively** — Camera Raw *Denoise* (Photo → Enhance) and AI *masking* (Select Subject / Sky) both work.
- **Edit in Photoshop** — round-trips correctly, and the submenu opens instantly (see the [ten-second menu](/wiki/neutron/fixes/edit-in-lag/) fix).
- **Copy Settings, masking popups, filmstrip scrolling** across large catalogs — all working.
- **Batch export.**

Remaining rough edges are cosmetic: the window can render off-center until the app finishes loading, panels come in a little slowly on launch, and the color-grading wheels and some scrollbars are slightly finicky. None block real work.

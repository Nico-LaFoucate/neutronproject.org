---
title: Plugins and extensions
status: feature
sub: Third-party panels and audio plugins, in daily use.
---

Third-party plugins are in daily use: Captioneer and other third-party panels in Premiere Pro, and VST3 audio plugins in Premiere Pro.

Most panels work. If one comes up blank, [report it](/wiki/start/help/report-a-problem/) with the panel's name; some need a per-vendor compatibility fix. If a panel that worked goes blank after the extension updates itself, run `neutron prefix provision <prefix>`, then restart the app.

The aescripts + aeplugins manager runs. Installing plugins through it into your Adobe apps hasn't been tested yet; [reports welcome](https://github.com/Nico-LaFoucate/Neutron/issues).

If a plugin won't activate, or keeps asking to be activated again, see [Troubleshooting](/wiki/start/help/troubleshooting/).

Neutron runs Adobe After Effects on Linux: After Effects 2026 and 2025, installed straight from Adobe and signed in with your own Adobe account. It's in beta, and it has been used for day-to-day work.

## What works

- **The composition viewer** renders, with its on-canvas overlays, rulers, guides and layer handles, at display refresh rate.
- **Dynamic Link** with Premiere Pro, in both directions. Comps update live across the link.
- **Plugins** from the shared plugin folder: Gyroflow, Boris FX Mocha and Satori appear in the **Effect** menu. See [Installing plugins](/wiki/start/basics/plugins/).
- **GPUs**: NVIDIA is the tested path. After Effects hasn't been tried on AMD or Intel GPUs yet.

## Get it running

[Install Collider](/wiki/start/basics/install-collider/), install After Effects from its Mud Hut tab, then see [Running your first app](/wiki/start/basics/first-app/). Current problems across the apps are listed under [Known issues](/wiki/start/help/known-issues/).

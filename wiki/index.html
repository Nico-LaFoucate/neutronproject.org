The stack

Neutron
Engine

The compatibility engine: Neutron-Wine, the graphics stack, and every Adobe-specific fix — all behind one CLI.

Collider
Launcher

The desktop launcher: finds your apps, checks the prefix is healthy, launches and supervises — and holds no compatibility logic of its own.

Mud Hut
Installer

The installer: gets Adobe apps onto your machine without hand-building a prefix. Ships inside Collider, and runs standalone in a terminal.

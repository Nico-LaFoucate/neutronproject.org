Press kit

Facts, screenshots and logos for writing about Neutron. Everything here has been tested. Checked October 28, 2026.

Neutron runs Adobe's Creative Cloud apps on Linux: Photoshop, Premiere Pro, After Effects, Illustrator, Lightroom Classic, Media Encoder and Animate. You install the apps straight from Adobe and sign in with your own account. Neutron is free, open source and in beta.

I'm Nico LaFoucate, I'm a creative of over 10 years. You might know me as one of the talents involved with Smack Studio on YouTube. I decided to swear off of Windows entirely two years ago because of its increasingly worse user experience. I have been a long-time tinkerer of Wine and a daily driver of Linux for the past two years. After I lost my full-time job, I needed to be able to use my own computer to do client work. It was out of this necessity that Neutron was born.

Nico LaFoucate, creator of Neutron

Fact sheet

Name
Neutron. Where the name could be confused with iZotope Neutron (an audio plugin) or OpenStack Neutron (cloud networking): “Neutron, the Adobe-on-Linux compatibility layer”.
Made by
Nico LaFoucate and Ficus Media Group. An independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Adobe.

October 8, 2026
Status
Beta
Price
Free. You need your own Adobe subscription.
Apps
Photoshop, Premiere Pro, After Effects, Illustrator, Lightroom Classic, Media Encoder and Animate, on Creative Cloud 2026 and 2025. Dynamic Link works both ways between Premiere Pro and After Effects. Audition and InDesign run on 2026. Lightroom (the cloud app) is planned. Status board
Used for paid client work
Premiere Pro, Photoshop, Lightroom Classic, After Effects and Media Encoder
Adobe builds tested
Each one listed with the date it was tested: Tested Adobe builds
The three parts
Neutron, the engine, with its own Wine runtime and a command line. Collider, the desktop app that sets everything up and launches the apps. Mud Hut, the installer that gets the apps from Adobe and keeps them updated.
How it's made
Neutron is built with assistance from AI tools. However, every change and fix is proposed, tested and signed off on by human hands.
Under the hood
Neutron's own Wine (Wine 11.10 with Staging and ntsync) carrying more than 260 patches written for these apps, plus DXVK and vkd3d-proton. Every patch is public and documented with the test that proved it.
Developed on
CachyOS, KDE Plasma (Wayland). Users also run it on Fedora, Ubuntu, Bazzite, Nobara and Arch, under GNOME and Hyprland.
Setup without a terminal
Tested on Fedora 44 (KDE Plasma), Ubuntu 26.04 (GNOME), Linux Mint 22.3 (Cinnamon) and Pop!_OS 24.04 (COSMIC)
GPUs
NVIDIA, AMD and Intel. On NVIDIA, Premiere Pro renders through CUDA and exports with NVENC. On AMD, Photoshop and Premiere Pro 2026 use the GPU. On Intel, Premiere Pro runs very well and Photoshop works on Iris Xe graphics.
Desktop sessions
Wayland. X11 sessions run with experimental support.
Pace
122 releases across Neutron, its Wine runtime, Collider and Mud Hut since October 8, and 25 reported issues closed (as of October 28, 2026)
Licenses
Neutron and its Wine runtime: LGPL-2.1-or-later. Collider and Mud Hut: Apache-2.0.
Source
Neutron · neutron-wine · Collider · Mud Hut

What it does

  • Installs and updates the apps straight from Adobe, with Adobe's own installer. The Creative Cloud app isn't needed. Updates keep your preferences, presets, plugins and sign-in, and the old version is kept in case anything fails.
  • Runs on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel GPUs. Photoshop and Premiere Pro use the GPU on NVIDIA and AMD. On NVIDIA, Premiere Pro renders through CUDA and exports with NVENC hardware encoding.
  • Pen tablets: pressure, tilt, the eraser and the pen's side buttons.
  • Two screens at different scales, such as a 175% main screen next to a 100% pen display.
  • Drag and drop from the file manager, and copy and paste images between the Adobe apps and Linux apps, both ways.
  • Plugins: effects, panels, Photoshop and Illustrator plugins, UXP plugins, VST3 audio plugins and vendors' own Windows installers.
  • Lightroom Classic imports from SD cards, and its masking, AI Denoise and Edit in Photoshop work.

The full list: What Neutron does today. What still goes wrong: Known issues.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't include any Adobe software, and it doesn't get around Adobe's licensing. You need your own Adobe subscription and sign in with your own account.
  • It doesn't modify Adobe's program files. Fixes go into Neutron's own Wine, so Adobe's next update doesn't undo them.
  • It isn't an Adobe product. Adobe doesn't make, support or endorse Neutron.

Known issues

Current problems across the apps, and what to do about them.

Only problems seen on current builds are listed here. Problems that have been fixed are under Notable fixes.

All apps

  • At a display scale that isn't a Windows step, parts of the interface land slightly off. In Premiere Pro, the marked in/out range on the timeline ruler drifts to the right of the selection. Set your desktop to 100, 125, 150, 175, 200, 225, 250 or 300% (see Choosing a display scale). This comes from inside the app, not from Neutron.

Animate

  • The home screen is sometimes blank. Start from File → New or File → Open. This is a bug that also happens on Windows.
  • File → New does nothing for about the first 15 seconds after launch. Wait, or try again after a moment. This comes from inside the app, not from Neutron.

Lightroom Classic

  • The interface takes several seconds to paint in when the app starts. Wait for it to finish. This comes from inside the app, not from Neutron.

Photoshop

  • The Camera Raw Filter window can open with its title bar above the top of the screen, so it can't be moved. Camera Raw still works: OK and Cancel are at the bottom, and F switches it to full screen. To get the title bar back, report it and we'll fix the saved window position for you.

Premiere Pro

  • Fast wheel-scrubbing on the timeline feels a bit late and jumpy. No workaround yet.
  • In Premiere Pro 2026, a sequence's thumbnail in the Project panel can show "Media pending" after you close a project and open it again. Hover over the thumbnail and the frame appears.
